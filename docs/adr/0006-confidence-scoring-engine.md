# ADR-0006: Add a Deterministic UFR Confidence Scoring Engine

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-04

## Context

KharchAI generates a Universal Financial Record (UFR) after document parsing
and validation. Downstream consumers need a consistent signal indicating
whether the extracted record should be trusted automatically or reviewed.

Confidence must be deterministic and must not be generated by the LLM. The
existing upload response is public and must remain backward compatible.

## Decision

Add `ConfidenceService` and run it in a new pipeline stage immediately after
UFR generation. The service calculates:

```text
confidence =
    image_quality_score       × 0.30
  + required_field_completeness × 0.30
  + validation_result           × 0.20
  + parser_confidence           × 0.20
```

The result is clamped to `0.0–1.0` and stored in UFR metadata:

- `confidence`
- `confidence_level`
- `review_required`

Levels are:

```text
HIGH   >= 0.90
GOOD   >= 0.80 and < 0.90
MEDIUM >= 0.60 and < 0.80
LOW    < 0.60
```

HIGH and GOOD records do not require review. MEDIUM and LOW records do.

Required fields are evaluated against the generated UFR and its item metadata,
with document-specific requirements for receipts, utility bills, and wallet
screenshots. Missing inputs contribute zero to their factor rather than being
treated as successful evidence.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Confidence is reproducible and independent of model self-assessment.
- Review routing can use one normalized score and explicit decision flag.
- Existing receipt, utility, and wallet extraction behavior is preserved.
- The public `ReceiptUploadResponse` remains unchanged.

### Limitations

- Quality failures still stop the existing upload pipeline before UFR
  generation; low-quality confidence behavior is testable through the
  deterministic service/report boundary.
- Required-field definitions are MVP rules and may evolve as parser coverage
  expands.
